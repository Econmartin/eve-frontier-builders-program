# EVE Vault

EVE Vault holds your Sui assets and acts as your identity when you connect to the game and to player-made dApps. You create it with your EVE Frontier sign-in through [zkLogin](zklogin.md), so there's no seed phrase to back up.

Install it only from the [official releases](https://github.com/evefrontier/evevault/releases). Read more in [Wallets & Identity](https://github.com/evefrontier/builder-documentation/blob/main/eve-vault/wallets-and-identity.md).
